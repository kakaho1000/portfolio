    <h3>2023-2025 Collegiate Tetris League (CTL)</h3>
    <p>Through CTL, I have hardened my belief that no event should ever be run the same twice in a row: there is always room for more growth, innovation, and creativity. Unlike other competitive games, TETR.IO is an individual, 1v1 game that requires zero team communication. It fundamentally juxtaposed my goal for the league: encouraging people worldwide make meaningful connections, and build their own local community. Thus, a unique 5v5 team format was born, playtested before the league's first year, and iterated upon with community feedback. Since 2023, CTL has grown to include 45+ universities, 500+ unique players, and is without a doubt a leading force in the rise of local in-person Tetris events across North America.</p>

    <h3>UBC Gaming Expo 2026</h3>
    <p> Although I contributed significantly to the logistics of this event, what I'm most proud of is my work on broadcast. I started learning in-person broadcast production half a year prior by showing up to every event, asking how I can provide help, and documenting rigorously. Since then, I've created comprehensive broadcast guides for 10+ events with lists of equipment, photos for setup, and flowcharts for wiring. At UBC Gaming Expo 2026, I independently set up the audio, streaming and visual equipment, coordinated with 10+ departments for each segment of the show, and streamed the event live.
