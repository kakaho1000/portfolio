### Goal 
- Integrate a 24V robotic hand with 5V-7.2V myoelectric prosthetic parts
- Allow passersby to operate the device using muscle signals at the Design & Innovation Day demo

### Personal responsibilities
- Led consultations with local prosthetics clinic to gain insight on user needs and current prosthetic technology 
- Led hardware validation and troubleshooting in an electronics laboratory, reporting to client through video calls
- Designed and 3D printed hand mount for demo

### Results
- Fully integrated system with custom slip-ring PCB, voltage step-down power delivery, control algorithm and LCD display
- 100% of interested passersby successfully activated the device
- Client was very satisfied with the delivered product
